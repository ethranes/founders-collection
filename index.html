Four cards. One story.

Each major story is a Connection: four cards, marked A to D, that tell it chapter by chapter. Collect the cards. Complete the Connections. Explore the place.

  1. Collect

    Open packs, swap your duplicates and gather the four chapters of each Connection. Every card has original artwork, and each front tells a different part of the story.

  2. Connect

    Turn a finished set of four over. The backs are the four quarters of one continuous picture, and joining them reveals it. That picture is your proof the Connection is complete.

  3. Discover

    Scan the QR code on the front of any card. Its page has three parts: Story for the full history, sources and artist, Explore for where to go, and Collection for your progress.

  4. Explore

    Every Connection has its own trail around Swansea or Gower: safe public places, old photos to compare, things to spot and short quizzes, with digital stamps and badges for completing them.

You never have to travel to finish the collection. Collecting the cards and exploring the trails are tracked separately, so collectors anywhere can complete the set.

No trail is ever locked behind rarity. Any card from a Connection opens its trail. You never need a rare or special card to explore the real place.

Honest about what's true

Places collect history, rumour and folklore in equal measure. Rather than pass everything off as fact, every card tells you which kind of story you're holding.

Fact

Backed by sources we can show you. The QR page lists them.

Story

Rooted in real events, but some details were added or changed as it was retold over the years.

Legend

Folklore, told as folklore. Part of the place's character, never presented as history.

Swansea is where it begins.

Swansea and Gower are home, and they're where this project started. The first release is Swansea • Abertawe: The Founders Collection, and it will always carry that name.

It covers the city's heroes, its industry, its legends, its shipwrecks, its landscapes and its hidden stories, researched carefully and drawn by artists who know the place.

We won't take the idea anywhere else until Swansea has shown it works.

Where we are

This is an early-stage project, and we'd rather be clear about that. Nothing is on sale yet. We're making one story properly first, so real collectors, shops and partners can hold it before anything else gets built.

  1. Done Concept and collection design

    The four-card structure, the joined backs, the story trails, the Story, Explore and Collection pages and the approach to research are all in place.

  2. Done Business support

    We've approached Business Wales and completed and sent their Starting and Running a Business workbook.

  3. Now One complete prototype Connection

    Four print-quality cards and their joined back, a pack mock-up, working Story, Explore and Collection pages, and at least one safe real-world trail activity to go with them.

  4. Next Testing with real people

    Putting the prototype in front of collectors, Swansea residents, visitors, families and young people. We want to know whether they understand the Connections, whether they'd swap, and whether the trails make them want to go out and explore.

  5. Then Partners and a small first run

    Taking the prototype to Swansea artists, museums, attractions and independent shops, opening an email waiting list, and making a small first print run only if the testing supports it.

  6. After that A decision based on evidence

    We'll only move to a full Founders Collection if the first run shows people want it.
