Four cards. One story.

Each major story is a Connection: four cards, marked A to D, that tell it chapter by chapter. The collection is meant to be completed, not endlessly chased.

  1. Collect

    Open packs and gather the four chapters of each Connection. Every card has original artwork by local artists.

  2. Connect

    Turn a finished set of four over. The backs join together to reveal a fifth image, and that image is your proof the Connection is complete.

  3. Discover

    Scan the QR code on any card to read the full story, see the sources behind it and meet the artist who drew it.

  4. Visit

    Every story points to a safe, public place in Swansea or Gower where you can see where it happened for yourself.

Honest about what's true

Places collect history, rumour and folklore in equal measure. Rather than pass everything off as fact, every card tells you which kind of story you're holding.

Fact

Backed by sources we can show you. The QR page lists them.

Story

Rooted in real events, but some details were added or changed as it was retold over the years.

Legend

Folklore, told as folklore. Part of the place's character, never presented as history.

Swansea is where it begins.

Swansea and Gower are home, and they're where this project started. The first release is Swansea • Abertawe: The Founders Collection, and it will always carry that name.

It covers the city's heroes, its industry, its legends, its shipwrecks, its landscapes and its hidden stories, researched carefully and drawn by artists who know the place.

We won't take the idea anywhere else until Swansea has shown it works.

Where we are

This is an early-stage project, and we'd rather be clear about that. Nothing is on sale yet. We're making one story properly first, so real collectors, shops and partners can hold it before anything else gets built.

  1. Done Concept and collection design

    The four-card structure, the joined backs, the digital story pages and the approach to research are all in place.

  2. Now One complete prototype Connection

    Four print-quality cards, their joined back, working QR story pages and a pack mock-up.

  3. Next Testing with real people

    Putting the prototype in front of collectors, Swansea residents, visitors, families, retailers and heritage organisations, and listening to what they say.

  4. Then A decision based on evidence

    We'll only move to a full Founders Collection if the testing shows people want it.
